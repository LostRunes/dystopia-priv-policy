Privacy Policy

Last updated · 23 October 2026

Dystopia (“the app”) is a single-player game made by Abinash Mohanty. This policy explains what data the app handles.

In short: the app collects no personal data. Your progress lives only on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (discoveries, story progress, quests, achievements, the player name and crest you choose, the world you are in, and your settings) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

Network access

The app uses an internet connection only to download the fonts it displays, from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com). As with any web request, Google’s servers receive your device’s IP address when the fonts are downloaded. No game data is sent with these requests. See Google’s privacy policy.

Permissions

The app requests three standard permissions:

  • Internet and network state, to download the fonts described above.
  • Wake lock, used by the video player while the animated backgrounds play.

It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
